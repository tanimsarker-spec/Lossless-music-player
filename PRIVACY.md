# Privacy

LOSSLESS is local-first. It has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and makes no network
requests. Music files are read in place or copied into the app's own Music folder. Listening
history, favorites, queue, EQ and settings are stored on the device only.

Permissions (Android): audio/media read access to scan your music; foreground service and wake lock
for background playback. No location, contacts, camera or microphone.

The diagnostic log in Settings stays in memory on the device.

If online lyrics or metadata are ever added, they will be opt-in and documented here first.
